# 0700000b — Tower C (executive floor + the roof, `UpdateMFX_03TowerC` 0x113a20)

The Tower mission's last level: from the outer balcony Bond works up to the executive floor,
plants the Q-Worm on Mayhew's computer, retrieves the NightFire files from the secure terminal,
escapes to the roof, takes the parachute from the helicopter and jumps. 51 maps, 242 objects, 380
placements, 10 drones, 177 collision cels [D level-survey].
