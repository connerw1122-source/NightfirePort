## The invisible wall after the intro (the missile cage) — fixed

The walkway from the shutters to the gondola crosses the collision of `BlockingPoly_PlayerMissile1/2/3` — a
**player-missile cage**: three 0x1000-flagged (not drawn, no mesh) objects whose triangles put a lid at y 52.8
over the whole map, a floor at y −40, a wall plane at x 135.3 (z −100.1..−116) and two walls over the station
walkway (z −100.1 × x 135.3–144, x 144) [D triangles]. The game's `Collide_Pick` (0x1eb320 disasm
0x1eb480-0x1eb49c) skips collision nodes whose cel flags word (cel+0x94 = the glist flags) has bit 0x1000 when
the query's HITTEST+0x98 carries bit 0x20 — the actor queries — while ray/sight queries still hit them; the
level is playable across that wall in the game. The port's static world took every triangle unconditionally, so
Bond hit an invisible wall at the x 135.3 plane on the walkway descent. Fixed: `world.rs` splits the 0x1000
objects into a missile-only set (solid to `segment` — bullets/sight/the floor rays; invisible to
`capsule_push`). Tests: `fix_objects2_live.rs` (the walk past the wall + the bullet/capsule sisters).
