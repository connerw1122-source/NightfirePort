## 3. The route

1. **Arrival** at the warehouse start; the start trigger #436 (out 0x23 [D]) fires under Bond →
   objective 3 ("Advance through Warehouse TR-2") completes and objective 4 reveals. `Mission_Init`
   (0x182270) preserves record 11 (the 0x1b/0x1c/0x23 channels ride the warm save) and resets
   record 12's channels [M 0x182270:92-121].
2. **ST-3**: through the yard gate to plate #378 → ch 0x1d → objective 4 complete, objectives 5–6
   revealed.
3. **The laser photograph**: the laser prototype's test-rig display in the ST-3 yard; a trench
   (floor 3.98) fronts the raised display floor (7.98 [D coll-floor]). The camera gadget (0x54,
   carried from 0700000c [M 0x1a7ef8 case 0x700000d keeps the loadout]) photographs the
   `CamSubject` → ch 0x1e.
4. **C5**: the double security door `bigdoor01`/`bigdoor02` (133–136, 5.67, −269.4) is a 0xE1
   `Destroy` pair (health 1000/10000 [D]); the mounted laser on the catwalk cuts it (the real
   game's sequence — see §6) → the death writes ch 0x1f → objective 6.
5. **The corridor**: the sloped corridor beyond C5 (floor 12.8 at z −260 down to 3.65 at z −222
   [D coll-floor]); its low stretch crosses plate #433 → ch 0x28 → the AND #434 completes the
   mission → 0x730000d.

## 4. Actors and mechanics

41 drones (skins 0x500064-weapon guards; the y ≈ 19.7 catwalk squad watches the crane bay [D
drone-props]); no allies or civilians. The crane sequence: `Overhead Crane_touchplate` #460 (out
ch 0x2b) arms `shipping_handle` #112 (a 0xE2 SimpleScript whose activate channel is param 3 =
0x2b [D; SS_Activate 0x1a9ea8 writes it on use — Player_Activate case 0x25]) → the script player
`ScriptPlayer_ShippingCrane` #115 (gate param 6 = 0x2b [D]) plays the shipping-crane script
0x060009EF; `crane_touchplate` #450 (out ch 0xa) gates `ScriptPlayer_CraneRotate` #492 (0x06000A08).
The zip wire `wire_crane2crane` #271 (0x3E) and the ladders #435/#449 (0x22) connect the cranes
(fix-wire's wire ride and fix-ladder's climb cover them).
