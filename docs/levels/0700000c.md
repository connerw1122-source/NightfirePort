## 3. The route

1. **Start** on the ST-1 walkway (y ≈ 14.6 floor).
2. **Down to the lower walkway** (floor 11.84 at z −154..−111 [D coll-floor]) and walk it: plate
   #506/#274 → ch 0x1b → objective 1 complete, objective 2 revealed.
3. **The photograph**: the jetpack prototype sits in the skylight display at the ST-1 end
   (`Camera_Subject` #414 (5.50, 7.55, −110.37) and `Camera_Subject_Big` #454 (5.32, 8.77, −109.14),
   the `Jetpack`/`jetpack-visor`/`projector` props #449–452 [D]). Bond equips the camera gadget
   (weapon 0x54, in the level loadout [M 0x1a7ef8 case 0x700000c]) and fires at the display; the
   bullet (class 5) entering the subject's glist writes ch 0x1c [M 0x1bc668]. The vent route
   (`ventmesh-front` #138, `VentLatch` #182 — a `Lock_Create` 0x1ae1f8 object — and the
   `obj_stor*_pulley` winches #351/#364/#377) is the way down; the port's walkthrough reaches the
   display from the ground floor.
4. **The exit**: the LOAD_LEVEL gate #556 (96.92, 12.62, −209.71; next level **0x0700000d** =
   param 0, key gate ch 0x1c = param 1 [D]) — USE-activated ([M Player_Activate 0x1a5170 case 0x1d →
   Trigger_Activate 0x1b2318; the trigger's own gate param 2 is 0, so `Trigger_Update` case 10 never
   self-fires [M 0x1b1a78:162-166]]). Bond walks the lower ground path (floor 10.34 [D coll-floor])
   to the gate plane and presses E: with ch 0x1c on, `Trigger_Activate` calls
   `ResetMap_LevelToLoad(0x0700000d, 1, 0)` [M 0x1b2318:111].
