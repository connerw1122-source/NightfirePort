# 0700000a — Tower B (the office floor, `UpdateMFX_03TowerB` 0x1137b0)

The Tower mission's second level: the lift drops Bond on the 40-something office floor of the
Phoenix Building; he plants the **Q-Worm** virus on the office computer systems, finds the
security center and unlocks the exterior (maintenance-hatch) door, and leaves for the outer
balcony. 38 maps, 601 objects, 800 placements, 20 drones, 543 collision cels [D level-survey].

## Route (spawn → Q-Worm PC → security keyboard → the hatch)

1. Spawn (−3.9, 0, 10.4) in the corridor outside the lifts (Room-M_lifts). Plate #58 (ch 0x47)
   completes obj3 at arrival [D].
2. The office floor is a loop of corridors (corridors_a_1..8, corridors_b_1..6) around offices
   A/B/C/D/G/I/J+K/N and the canteen (Office-Z) [D survey names]. Guarded by 20 drones [D
   drone-props]. Many doors lock on channels: the door-unlock muxes OR(ch 0x13/0x15/0x18/0x1a —
   drone-side plates, mask 0x10, ch 0x37) → ch 0x14/0x16/0x19/0x1b [D trig dump #632-635] — a
   guard touching his side plate (or the ch-0x37 state) unlocks the office doors.
3. **The Q-Worm PC** (−16.9, 1.2, −32.2) sits in the south-west office area near the
   Vent_StoreRoom ladder (0x22 at (−13.2, −1.3, −9.5)) and the oDroneTrigger3 room. Bond equips
   the **Q-Worm gadget (weapon 0x58, [L weapon-data table] + [M 0x1aee20's 0x58 test])** and
   fires it at the PC → the install runs (the keycode message, Txt_BindLabel 0x2000014, ~180
   ticks [M 0x1aee20]) → **ch 0x48** → obj4.
4. **The security center** (Office-N-security room, the oDroneTrigger1 room at (−8…−4, 1.3,
   13…15)): the keyboard SP #60 (mask 4) plays script 0x06000597 (25 ticks: indicator entities
   0x020005E1/E2, keep-objects) → out **ch 0x3A** held [D nis dump + M 0x1dbda8]. AND #741 →
   **ch 0x49** → obj5. The neighbour switch #643 "SCRIPTPLAYER_KeyboardLasers" (0x29, ch 0x3B,
   gate ch 0x3C, animation 0x06000624) drives the keyboard-laser show (SCRIPT_KeyboardLasers_ON/
   DURING) — its gate ch 0x3C has no writer in the dumps (open question below).
5. **The exit**: LOAD_LEVEL #59 "Load Point" (21.72, 1.12, 19.79), next 0x0700000b, **key_gate =
   ch 0x4A**, gate 0 [D]. The M.Hatch indicators (locked #679 / unlocked #680, 0x31 art) sit at
   the hatch. TOUCH_ONCE #757 (out ch 0xB7, lock ch 0x3A) at [19.78, 0, 18.37]-[23.37, 1.40,
   19.77] — an arrival/voice plate active once the keyboard is done. With ch 0x4A on (Q-Worm +
   keyboard), E at the hatch loads 0700000b [M 0x1b2318:76-90].

## The laser web (defence, not the route)

The 0xEB TOUCH triggers "laser"/"laserbeam" (#606-#662) are the office laser grid: mask 8 (the
player), out ch 0x1F/0x20/0x21/0x22/0x24/0x25/0x26/0x13/0x15/0x18/0x1A, **lock ch 0x37** [D].
Muxes #667-670 OR them into ch 0x0F-0x12, #782 into ch 0x81 — the guard-alert/music channels
(the MusicTrigger family). ch 0x37 has no writer in this level's dumps [D]; it likely arrives
carried from 07000009's laser switch (#71 there toggles ch 0x37) — [S, unverified].

  the object matrix from the script) — the port activates it with its placement-matrix fallback;

  07000009's elevator).
- **Switch animations / the keyboard-laser show** (0x06000624): presentation.
- **SimpleScript** Vent_SecurityArea / room-M_LIFTDOOR1/2 (0xE2): the lift doors RESOLVED (task
  710a) — their state param is 1 (armed [D]), so `SS_Update` 0x1a9948's state-1 sweep runs at
  create and the wrap (wrap_flag 1) holds the open end pose [M, grates-02's state model]; the
  walked route leaves through the open cab mouth (the old "drawn at rest" note predates the
  walkreal un-ignore). Vent_SecurityArea stays drawn/E-armed (mask 4, state 0x40 [D]).
- **Monitor/GT (gun turret) classes**: none on this level's route (0x27 ×6 = GT_Create gun
  turrets, [M parsemap case 0x27] — sky-bridge set pieces, out of the walkthrough).
