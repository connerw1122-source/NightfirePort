# 07000009 — Night of the Agent (Tower A, `UpdateMFX_03TowerA` 0x113558)

The Tower mission's first level: Bond infiltrates the Phoenix Building's underground car park at
night in the rain and reaches the main lift to the office floor. 40 maps, 151 objects, 288
placements, 6 drones, 120 collision cels [D `nf-extract level-survey`]. The music updater is keyed
0x07000006 too (`UpdateMFX_03TowerA` reads `*(int*)MapMusicData != 0x07000006` [M 0x113558:36]).

## The elevator activation — SOLVED (elevator-buttons-09)

The button is **not** a placement-side object at all; the use target is a **script entity**:

- `SCRIPTPLAYER_Elevators` #70 (0xD9, hash 0x0600059B, mode 64, stay 1, mask 4, out ch 57) has its
  art baked absolute in the model: gfx bounds [−18.382, −368.534, 24.832]..[−17.775, −367.921,
  25.198] [D placements dump] — a small panel in the lobby by the BodyArmourHeavy, 15 units from the
  lift shaft. The earlier "the SP object IS the animated cab" reading was wrong: script 0x0600059B
  is a 25-tick activation FX (not the ride) — stream 0 creates entities 0x020005EA (t=0) and
  0x020005EB (t=16) (obj_type 0x1F → class 0), stream 2 plays sound 0x4A1 at t=16, t=25 EntityEnd
  [D nis dump].
- `Player_Activate` 0x1a5170:121-122 reaches `SP_Activate` **only** on a hit object with
  `obj+0xE4 != 0 && class == 0` — a script entity (`Script_EntityStart` 0x1d8d78:108-110 stores the
  owner SP there). The class-0x1B SP object itself is inert to the pick (no case; obj+0xE4 = 0
  [L ee_start_05 ×8]). The earlier "the E path cannot reach #70 either" conclusion mis-read the arm:
  it fires on the SP's ENTITIES, not on the SP.
- The mode-64 preload (play+update+stop + one more `Script_Update` [M 0x1dbc08:41-56]) runs the
  t=0 ops, so entity A exists at the panel from level load (`Control_BuildWorldSph` gives it the
  model's world-baked sphere). E near the panel → latch → state 1→2 → `switch_channels[57] = 1`
  [M 0x1dbda8:107-123, 221-229] → the AND mux #274 (objective 2) and the LIFTDOORS (below). The
  script then plays the FX (entity B + sound 0x4A1 at t=16), EntityEnd at t=25, and stay=1 deletes
  the SP (one-shot button) [M 0x1dbda8:142-159, 190-192].
- The cab ScriptPlayers #21/#22 ("SCRIPT_liftSCRIPTPLAYER", loop scripts 0x060005FE/0x060005FD, mask
  4, out 0 [D placements]) are NOT the activation: their out channel is 0, so an E on their objects
  only restarts their idle loop and writes nothing [M 0x1dbda8:221-229 with out = 0]. Their
  placements sit at y = 0 (the art is positioned by their loop scripts' anim sequences).

Port: the entity-sphere use path is implemented in `useobj::UseObjects::player_activate` +
`nis::Player::entity_use_targets` (docs/systems/elevator-button.md). The walkthrough below drives
the real E press.

## Actors

6 drones (type 0x0f), all `k5 0x64` (NEW behaviour), k15 = 13, k16 = 0 (no death channel), normal
alertness 0 / alert block 1 [D drone-props]:
- Car park: (−3.9, −376.0, 36.0) — right by the start.
- Lobby: (1.6, −368.3, 22.3), (−10.5, −368.3, 80.2), (−0.3, −368.3, 68.0), (5.2, −368.3, 45.0),
  (23.8, −368.3, 33.1).
The drones' placement param 3 = 0x51 — their WAIT_SWITCH channel is the ARRIVAL TOUCH PLATE
#248's out channel [D drone params; M `NDrone2_DSTATE_Global` 0x15b830 waits while the channel is
off]: the plate fires on the first collide pass and wakes them (`NDrone2_DSTATE_WaitSwitch`
0x15b960 case 3 — the channel EVENT reaches a frozen drone). The port needs no special path for
that: the game's wake poll rides the drone's OWN control run — `NDrone2_ControlSTANDARD`
0x1490f0:81 self-sends msg 3 every frame and `Drone_Control` 0x139268 gates only on
NPCGlobals/`Drone_bDisableSystem` (no enable-flag gate) — so the port's per-drone walk slot
samples `channel_on` at the slot (the game's newest-first object order, 5aafe03d) and the
WAIT_SWITCH msg::UPDATE arm wakes the disabled waiter exactly there. An earlier attempt
(level-09-interactables, held) sampled the channels for the waiting frozen drones at the TICK
HEAD instead: that out-of-order poll saw pulses the game's own update order hides (07000005's
mux 270 rewrites 0xA4 before drones 65–68 update — ai_live's alarm-pulse test) and broke four
master tests, and its frame-1 wake on 09 landed before the intro's start path, whose machinery
then re-entered WaitSwitch on the woken drones (the "re-freeze" the held task reported: an
artifact of the early wake, gone once the loop was dropped — all six ch-0x51 drones sit enabled
through the intro). The guards are killable: the kill test's zero damage was its own weapon —
09's loadout opens on the K5 sleep darts (0x43, 5+10 [L loadout trace]) whose hits are the
zero-damage dart msg [M DealWithObjHit case 5 0x13fff0:269-306: 0x43/0x44 → msg 0x19], so the
shooter cycles to the P2K (0x2) and the guard dies to the real fire path (hp 10 → −1.8, corpse).
Nothing in the port writes ch 0x7C/0x7D (grep: no writer in crates/nf-game) — the alarm switches
are the writers (class-0x39 Switch objects [M Switch_Activate 0x1b02a8]).

## Not ported / gaps (with the game function)

- **SimpleScript playback** (0xE2 LIFTDOORS_*_ENDLEVELDOORS animation): `SS_Update` 0x1a9948 /
  `SS_Activate` 0x1a9ea8 — the port models the state machine, the channel gate and the posed
  collision (ss.rs; the doors are solid through `script_parts`) and the walk test proves the
  channel-gate open; the door-open ANIMATION itself is a draw-side item (the main agent renders).
- **Monitor camera view** (Monitor_Activate 0x1b0058 / Monitor_Update 0x1afcc8): presentation only.
- **FuseBox shooting** (FuseBox_Update 0x1adfe8): no fused boxes with channels on this level.
- **Switch animations** (the switch SPs Switch_Create 0x1adcc0 attaches): the port toggles the
  channel without the button animation.
- **The E route through the closed lift doors**: the port's use pick runs from the RENDER CAMERA
  while the game builds it from `Player_GetHeadPos` [M 0x1a5170:101-106] — at the doors the
  camera hangs ~2.5 behind Bond and the 1.0 sphere never spans the exit box (z 10.33..12.49 [D
  #63]). Fix candidate for its own session (the doors/switches/SP reaches are tuned to the
  camera); the elevator route is the pinned exit. Live check in the report.
